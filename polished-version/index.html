SECURITY ARCHITECTURE

Privacy starts on your device.

SecureShare encrypts files in your browser using AES-256-GCM. Only encrypted data is temporarily stored on the transfer server. Your password and encryption key never leave your device.

Switch to Decrypt mode, select the .encrypted file, enter the original password, and click Decrypt File.

The password field is cleared after an operation and whenever you switch modes.

AES-GCM authentication fails if the password is incorrect or the encrypted file has been altered.
